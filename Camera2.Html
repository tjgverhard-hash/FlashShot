Full-frame image quality

A full-frame sensor is a camera sensor that is the same size as traditional 35mm film. It captures more light and detail, gives better low-light performance, and produces a natural background blur shallow depth of field, which is often seen as more professional looking in photos and video.

Improved autofocus with subject detection

The autofocus is improved and more intelligent, with subject detection for people, animals, and vehicles. It's accurate and dependable in most situations, though not as extremely fast as top sports-focused cameras.

Reliable battery life

The battery is reliable and long-lasting, especially for a full-frame camera. You can comfortably shoot a full day of casual photography without needing constant recharging. It's well-suited for travel and everyday use.
